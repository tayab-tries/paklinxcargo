# Air Freight Cargo Solutions

Air freight represents the fastest method for moving international shipments. Connecting major commercial export airports in Pakistan (such as Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad) with worldwide air terminals, air cargo services support critical supply chain deadlines.

## Cargo Preparation & Packaging

Proper packaging is vital for air transit safety. Shipments should be enclosed in durable double-walled corrugated boxes or reinforced crates, with clear address labeling and airway bill documentation.
