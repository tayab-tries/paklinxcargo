# Commercial Cargo Logistics

Pakistan's export sector relies on robust international commercial cargo services to ship manufactured goods, textiles, agricultural products, and surgical instruments to international trade hubs.

## Industrial Packaging & Palletization

Commercial freight should be secured on standard heat-treated pallets or enclosed wooden crates to enable safe forklift loading and airport/port terminal handling.
