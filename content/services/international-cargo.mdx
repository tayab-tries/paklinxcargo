# International Cargo Shipping Overview

International cargo logistics encompasses the structured transport of goods across international borders. Operating from major logistics centers across Pakistan, international cargo services enable commercial exporters and individual shippers to transport items efficiently worldwide.

## Transport Modes & Cargo Options

Depending on urgency, volume, and budget, international shipping is handled through specialized modes:

- **Air Freight**: Optimal for time-sensitive cargo and smaller high-value shipments.
- **Sea Cargo**: Suitable for heavy, bulk, or large-volume containerized freight.
- **Door-to-Door**: Comprehensive coordination from initial collection point to final destination address.

## Documentation & Customs Compliance

Every international shipment requires proper documentation, including commercial invoices, packing lists, and standard export declarations. Compliance with destination country customs regulations ensures smooth clearance upon arrival.
