# Door-to-Door Cargo Shipping

Door-to-door logistics simplifies international shipping by combining local collection, export clearance, international freight transit, and final delivery into one seamless process.

## Service Benefits

By managing every leg of the shipping chain, door-to-door services eliminate the need for recipients to arrange terminal pickup at overseas airports or sea ports.
