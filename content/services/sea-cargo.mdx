# Sea Cargo & Ocean Freight Overview

Ocean shipping is the cornerstone of global trade, carrying bulk merchandise and commercial products across international waters. Operating through port access in Pakistan, sea cargo services facilitate bulk commodity and commercial export trade.

## Container & Cargo Handling

Cargo is loaded into marine containers designed to withstand maritime weather conditions. Heavy items must be adequately blocked, braced, and weight-distributed within crates or pallets to prevent movement during voyage transit.
